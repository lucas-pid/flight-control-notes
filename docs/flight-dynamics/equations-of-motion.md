# Equations of Motion

## Translational Equations of Motion

$$\sum \boldsymbol{F} = m \cdot \left\{ \left(\dot{\boldsymbol{V}}^{R}\right)^{EB} + (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{EB}) \times (\boldsymbol{V}^{R})^{E} + 2 \cdot (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IE}) \times (\boldsymbol{V}^{R})^{E} + (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IE}) \times \left[ (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IE}) \times (\boldsymbol{r}^{R}) \right] \right\} + m \cdot \left\{ \left(\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}^{IB}\right)^{B} \times (\boldsymbol{r}^{RG}) + (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IB}) \times \left[ (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IB}) \times (\boldsymbol{r}^{RG}) \right] \right\}$$

## Rotational Equations of Motion

$$\sum (\boldsymbol{M}^{R}) = \boldsymbol{I}^{R} \cdot (\dot{\boldsymbol{\omega}}^{IB})^{B} + (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IB}) \times [\boldsymbol{I}^{R} \cdot (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IB})] + m \cdot (\boldsymbol{r}^{RG}) \times \left[ (\dot{\boldsymbol{V}}^{R})^{IB} + (\boldsymbol{\omega}^{IB}) \times (\boldsymbol{V}^{R})^{I} \right]$$
